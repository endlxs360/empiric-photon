The starting point

A professional services consultancy spent a large part of the week drafting reports, proposals and meeting minutes from scattered notes, and fixing the formatting of each document in Word. The work was repetitive and quality varied depending on who wrote it.

What we built

  1. One Claude Master Prompt for each type of document, with a fixed output schema.
  2. A Python script that validates the response, completes the data and assembles the content.
  3. A corporate .docx template that applies styles and layout automatically.

The result

The team freed up around 15 hours a week. We measured it by comparing the hours logged on drafting and formatting before and after putting the workflow into operation. In addition, documents come out looking the same every time, and reviews focus on content.

What we learned

  • It pays to start with a single document type and expand later.
  • A good template contributes more than a very long prompt.
  • Human review is still necessary: automation speeds up the draft, it does not replace judgement.

Result of a specific case. Actual savings depend on each team's volume and document types.
