What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device to remember information about your visit. They are used to make the site work, to measure its use and to display advertising.

Types of cookies this site may use

  • Technical or necessary: they let the site work and remember basic choices. They do not require consent.
  • Analytics: they measure how the site is used so we can improve it. They are only activated with your consent.
  • Advertising: installed by Google AdSense and its partners to display ads and measure their performance, including personalisation based on your browsing. They are only activated with your consent.
  • Linked third parties: if you go to Gumroad or X from this site, those services may install their own cookies under their own policies.
